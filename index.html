About the Publishing House

Crafting Books That Shape Young Imaginations.

Carmel Publications is an educational publishing house committed to producing visually rich, pedagogically sound, and artistically inspiring books for young learners.

Based in Mulanthuruthy, Ernakulam, Kerala, Carmel Publications specializes in pre-primary and early primary curriculum materials. Each book is designed from the child's perspective — combining vibrant full-color offset printing, tactile paper stocks, and developmental learning ladders that make education a joyful adventure.

Premium Print Integrity

Heavyweight, tear-resistant paper and non-toxic soy-based ink printing.

Pedagogical Rigor

Graded progression matching early developmental and motor milestones.
