About the publisher

Books that shape young imaginations.

Carmel Publications was established in 2020 with the goal of providing high-quality, beautifully illustrated books to children in pre-primary and primary classes across the country.

We are a team of educators, writers and designers dedicated to creating the best learning materials for young students, parents and teachers. We believe that every child has the potential to learn and grow, and it is our mission to provide the educational tools and resources necessary to help them succeed.

We specialise in carefully graded books for Nursery to Grade 8 — early childhood and alphabet, mathematics and numbers, handwriting and penmanship, Hindi language, rhymes and poetry, and art, craft and creativity. Our books are written to support the NEP and NCF framework and are available in English and Hindi. Our team keeps up with the latest teaching approaches and educational trends, so that our materials stay relevant and effective for today's learners.

Carmel Publications was founded on the principles of accessibility, inclusiveness and innovation. We are constantly exploring new technologies and approaches to education, and we are always looking for ways to improve and evolve our books. We are grateful for the opportunity to serve in the field of education and to make a positive impact on the lives of children.

Our aim

To make learning joyful, accessible and inclusive for every young child — by publishing high-quality, innovative books that inspire curiosity, build strong foundations and help students achieve their full potential.
